LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

At WebToApp Studio Pro (accessible at webtoapp.io), the privacy of our visitors and creators is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal and non-personal information collected, recorded, and utilized by our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • User-Provided Information: When you register an account, we may collect your username and email address to manage your account and authenticate access to your created apps.
  • Target URL & Customization Configurations: When you submit a URL or HTML package to be packaged into an application, our automated server analyzes the page structure (metadata title, icon, viewport) solely for generating the requested application package.
  • Log Files & Analytics: Like standard web platforms, we log non-personally identifiable diagnostic data such as browser type, referring pages, timestamp, and IP address for security, abuse prevention, and rate limiting.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our multi-platform app generation services.
  • Generate downloadable package containers (Android APKs, iOS WebClips, Windows launchers).
  • Understand and analyze platform usage to improve performance and compatibility.
  • Prevent fraud, bot attacks, and illegal content distribution.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie (DART cookie), to serve ads to our site visitors based on their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

WebToApp uses standard browser cookies and local storage to store user authentication sessions (tokens) and local build history for your convenience. You can configure your browser to decline cookies, though certain features (such as user account persistence) may be impaired.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of any inaccuracies, request erasure of your data, or restrict processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via our Contact Us page.

6. Security of Your Data

We employ modern industry-standard cryptographic techniques (TLS 1.2/1.3 encryption, salted password hashing, and isolated signing keystores) to protect your information and generated application packages.
